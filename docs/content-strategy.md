# Content Strategy

## Audience

- small businesses
- agencies
- consultants
- local businesses
- early-stage teams

## Message hierarchy

1. what the product does
2. why the audit matters
3. what the report surfaces
4. how the user should act on it

## Brief framing

- avoid legal-heavy copy unless the user is looking at a specific compliance issue
- keep the product language useful for small businesses and agencies
- present audit findings as practical signals, not scare copy
- prefer "here is what to fix" over "here is what went wrong"

## Copy principles

- keep sentences short and specific
- prefer plain language over compliance jargon
- surface evidence and outcomes, not vague claims
- make findings feel useful rather than alarming

## Content modules

- landing hero
- audit lenses overview
- report summary
- issue list
- severity and priority markers
- recommended next steps

## Report content model

- site name
- overall posture
- priority areas
- per-lens findings
- recommended next actions
- confidence or completeness notes when relevant

## Tone examples

- "Consent timing needs attention."
- "The page hierarchy is clear but uneven."
- "Keyboard focus is present, but weak in contrast."
- "This site is usable, but the path to action can be cleaner."
