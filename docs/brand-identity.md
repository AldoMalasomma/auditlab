# Brand Identity

## Positioning

AuditLab should feel like a real SaaS product that helps teams understand website quality quickly.

## Brand traits

- calm
- precise
- technically trustworthy
- premium without being flashy
- clear without sounding legalistic

## Voice

- concise
- direct
- factual
- helpful

## Visual direction

- dark, polished interface with restrained contrast
- strong hierarchy and generous spacing
- subtle borders and depth instead of loud decoration
- wide-screen compositions that feel cinematic, not stretched

## UX expectations

- prioritise readability on mobile first
- keep primary actions obvious and limited
- let desktop layouts add width and depth without becoming crowded
- use calm status colors and clear hierarchy over decorative effects

## What to avoid

- generic template styling
- courtroom or compliance-dashboard clichés
- noisy motion
- excessive glassmorphism
- overpromising language
